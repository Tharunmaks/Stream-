Q: What is Expert Stream?
A: Expert Stream is a CPU engine that streams model weights from disk so big models run on small devices.

Q: What does LoRA train?
A: LoRA trains small low-rank matrices added to a frozen model, so only a few million numbers change.

Q: Who built Expert Stream?
A: Tharun built Expert Stream.

Q: Can I train on my phone?
A: Yes. es_lora runs on a phone CPU with Termux, using about 300 MB in low-RAM mode.

Q: What file does training produce?
A: Training produces a .eslora adapter, which can also be exported as a llama.cpp LoRA GGUF.
