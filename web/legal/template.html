Privacy, terms and the 20 launch checks

Last updated @@UPDATED@@. Plain words, no tracking, nothing hidden. This is written by the project, not by a lawyer; see the note at the end.

Privacy policy

Short version: ExpertStream collects nothing about you. It is a static web page plus programs that run on your own device. We run no server, no database, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party scripts.

Who is responsible

The operator named under Who runs this.

What stays on your device

The page stores these in your own browser only (see the exact list under Cookies and storage): your settings, prompt history, saved model files, the Hugging Face token if you choose to connect, and a small offline helper (service worker). You can delete all of it with one button, see Delete my data.

Other parties you may reach through the page

  • GitHub Pages hosts this page. Like any web host it can see technical request data such as your IP address and browser type, under GitHub's privacy statement. The operator does not receive or use that data.
  • Hugging Face (huggingface.co) is contacted only when you search for models, sign in or download. Your browser then sends requests, including your IP address and, if you connected, your access token, to Hugging Face under its privacy policy. Sign-in uses OAuth 2 with PKCE and asks for the scopes openid profile read-repos only: your profile name and read access to repositories so private or gated models can be downloaded. The page never sees your password. "Disconnect" removes the token.
  • Your own devices: the optional MCP server and native engine run on your phone or computer. If you start the server with --public, a tunnel provider you choose (for example Cloudflare or an SSH host) carries the traffic; the link contains a secret, treat it like a password.
  • Models you download come with their own licences and may behave in ways the operator does not control.

Legal basis, retention, sharing

Because the operator does not receive personal data, there is nothing to share, sell or retain. Data on your device stays until you delete it or clear your browser data. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling or automated decisions about you.

Your rights

Under laws such as GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and India's DPDP Act you may ask what data is held about you, to correct it, to delete it or to take it with you. All data is on your device, so you already hold it and can delete it yourself. If you believe the operator holds anything about you, write to the contact below and it will be answered within 30 days.

Children

The page is not directed at children under 13, and the operator knowingly collects no data from anyone, including children. Under 18 (or the age of majority where you live) please use it with a parent or guardian's permission, especially before connecting accounts or downloading models.

Changes are published here with a new date and are visible in the public change history of the repository.

Refund policy

ExpertStream is free, so there is nothing to pay for and nothing to refund. It never asks for a card or any payment details. If anyone asks you for money in the name of ExpertStream, it is not us; tell us.

Cookies and storage

Cookies: none. No analytics, advertising or tracking technology of any kind. Because no non-essential cookies or trackers exist, there is nothing to opt in to, so the page shows a plain privacy notice instead of a consent wall. Delete my data

Open Settings, then Privacy, then "Delete all my data" on the main page. It removes the settings, history, saved models, Hugging Face token and offline helper from this browser (the helper is set up again the next time you open the page). Clearing your browser's site data does the same. Because the operator holds no copy, there is nothing else to erase. To revoke the Hugging Face connection completely, also remove ExpertStream under huggingface.co/settings/connected-applications.

Accessibility

The goal is WCAG 2.2 level AA. What has been done: every control is a real button or link or has a role, tab stop and Enter/Space handling; a "Skip to content" link; a visible focus ring; dialogs close with Escape and return focus; text contrast of at least 4.5:1 (checked against the page's colour tokens); decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers; the site has no photographs, so no image needs alt text, and the one avatar image from Hugging Face is decorative; animations respect "reduce motion"; the layout works at phone width and when zoomed.

Known gaps: the in-page terminal and the animated intro graphic are visual and only partly usable with a screen reader. If something blocks you, tell us and it will be fixed.
